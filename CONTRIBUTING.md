# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the Hess Truck Repair website.

## Workflow

1. Fork or clone the repository.
2. Create a focused branch for your change.
3. Run `node server.cjs` and review the site at `http://127.0.0.1:4173`.
4. Check the affected section on desktop and mobile widths.
5. Open a pull request describing what changed and why.

## Content guidelines

- Keep the cream and deep-green visual system; do not add orange accents.
- Use plain, direct language for truck drivers, owner-operators, farmers, and fleet managers.
- Keep the phone CTA prominent and accessible.
- Use real Hess Truck Repair photography when adding images.
- Optimize images before committing them and write useful alternative text.
- Do not publish private customer information or unapproved testimonials.
- Confirm business hours, service details, and contact information with the owners before changing them.

## Review checklist

- The page works without a build step.
- Links, phone buttons, and the quote form still work.
- Text remains readable at mobile and desktop sizes.
- Images have descriptive `alt` text and do not distort.
- The change does not introduce orange, neon colors, gradients, or unnecessary animation.
